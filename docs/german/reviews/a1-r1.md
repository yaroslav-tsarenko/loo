**Problems that run across several topics**
- [major] **Cyrillic for h is inconsistent and fights the h ≠ г rule.** Topic 01 writes H as «га» (l.51, 62). Topics 07, 10 and 11 write h as «х» («хьорен» 07 l.198, «хойзер» 10 l.491, «хунт» / «хаст» 11 l.324, 338). Pick one convention, preferably a light «х» or plain "видих [h]", and never «г».
- [minor] **Umlauts written in Cyrillic, against §2.7.1:**

  - 07: «хьорен» l.198
  - 10: «льо-ве» l.186. In the umlaut topic this one is major, because it teaches exactly the ö = «йо» error.
