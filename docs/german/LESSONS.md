# Lessons from the wave 1 (A1) reviews — read before writing

Mistakes the opus reviewers found again and again in A1. Avoid them; reviewers will look for exactly these.

**Rules stated too absolutely** (§2.7.14). These were all wrong:
- «-chen завжди das» — der Kuchen;
- «-ieren ніколи не відокремлюється» — anprobieren;
- «die у множині завжди» — only Nom/Akk;
- «Er ist ein Lehrer» — a profession takes no article;
- «z завжди [ts]».
Before writing «завжди», «ніколи» or «лише», look for a counterexample at your level.

**Correct German marked as an error.** «Ich werde morgen kommen», «Mein Haar ist lang» and «Ich bin 30 Jahre» (colloquial) were all struck out. Only strike out what DWDS/Duden or a grammar would call wrong. Otherwise write «звучить неприродно / у нормі …».

**Quizzes with two defensible answers** — the most frequent major issue:
- a `bank` distractor that fits the gap grammatically and semantically (`bezahlen` in «Wir ___ zwei Kaffee», `kein` in «Es gibt ___ Fenster»);
- capitalisation in the bank giving the answer away;
- a «choose the correct sentence» item where a second option is also grammatical.

For every quiz, try each wrong option in the gap and ask whether a native speaker would accept it. Add Ukrainian glosses or infinitives in the stem when needed.

**Stress and IPA.**
- -zehn is unstressed: dreizehn is [ˈdʁaɪ̯tseːn].
- Information is stressed on -on.
- Use ɔʏ̯ (not ɔɪ̯), no ˌ in short words, and verb `forms:` that start with the infinitive.

**Cyrillic hints** (§2.7.15–17):
- h is «видих» / a light «х», never «г»;
- final g = «к» (Tag — «так»);
- no «є», «ьо» or «ә» for German vowels;
- «е» as in «день».

**Russianisms and surzhyk.** Wrong: по-німецьки, лес, жилля, творог, у Нади, де ти йдеш (→ куди), тримісна (→ трикімнатна), склоподібний (→ складотворчий), облажатися. Write natural literary Ukrainian.

**Facts.** These came out wrong:
- «Herbst ist regnerisch» — summer is wettest;
- «Hören звучить двічі» — part 2 of Start Deutsch 1 is played once;
- Sunday bakery hours — they depend on the Land;
- deposit wording — a lower deposit is valid.

Every Landeskunde number needs a source. If you can't source it, say «зазвичай / за різними оцінками».

**Level drift.**
- Card `level:` is the word's real CEFR level. Vokal, Silbe, Schmetterling and Staatsangehörigkeit are not A1.
- Landeskunde texts must stay within length: language topics A1 40–60, A2 80–120, B1 150–200, B2 200–280 words; Deutschland chapters A2 ≈ 100–140, B1 ≈ 120–160, B2 ≈ 160–220.

**Duplicate cards.** Before every `[!word]`, grep `content/Guides/Deutsch*`. The lower topic number keeps the card (§2.6 п. 10a).

**Joke distractors.** Don't use them: «Hallo Welt», «70 кімнат», «mit dem schwarzen Bus». Every distractor must be a real mistake a Ukrainian learner makes.

**`[!pair]`:** `say:` must exactly match one of the two words in the header (an article in the header breaks it).

## Added after waves 5–7 (B1, Deutschland B1, B2)

**Fill `bank:`** holds ONLY distractors — the parser adds the answers itself; listing them shows each answer chip twice.

**Open fills without a bank** almost always accept two answers (*solle/müsse*, *gelebt/gewohnt*, *Zuerst/Nun*). Add an infinitive or Ukrainian gloss in brackets, or a bank. **Order quizzes:** if a second word order is just as natural, merge chunks so only one order is possible.

**Verb `forms:`** start with the infinitive: `erklären → erklärte → hat erklärt` (not `erklärt → …`). Reflexive: `sich distanzieren → …`.

**Card `level:`** = the word's real CEFR level, not the topic's level. Theater, Partei, Stress, obwohl, Diktatur… are not B2 just because a B2 topic cards them.

**Unicode:** Ukrainian apostrophe is ʼ (U+02BC), never `'` or `’`. Write files with an editor or Python — `perl -pe` with a literal non-ASCII character writes mojibake («Ê¼»).

**IPA traps seen:** unstressed über- is [yːbɐ] (long); Premiere [pʁəˈmi̯eːʁə]; hoch [hoːx]; Master [ˈmaːstɐ]; Absatz [ˈapzats]; telefonieren stresses -nie-; ʔ after prefixes and in compounds (Burn-out [ˈbœːɐ̯nʔaʊ̯t]).

**Facts:** numbers that sources disagree on are given as a range with both sources. Fast-moving figures carry «станом на <місяць рік>». Legal notes (BGB, StVZO) are quoted from gesetze-im-internet.de and framed as orientation, not legal advice. Check reforms: Bundestag electoral law 2023 (Zweitstimmendeckung) + BVerfG 30.07.2024 on the Grundmandatsklausel.

**Exam formats:** use the current modular Goethe formats (Modellsatz on goethe.de); the old «Prüfungsziele» PDFs describe pre-2019 formats.

**Audio:** `[!listen]` audio ≤ 300 characters — split long monologues into consecutive blocks with a shared title, one question each. TTS reads standard German: don't promise dialect sounds the recording can't contain.

**Landeskunde lengths (prose only):** B2 200–280 words, C1 350–450, C2 450–600; Deutschland B2 ≈ 160–220.
